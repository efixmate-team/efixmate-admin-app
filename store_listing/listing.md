## Full description (max 4000)
eFixMate Admin is the official operations app for the eFixMate home-services team. It gives authorised administrators the full eFixMate admin panel on their phone, so bookings, technicians and payments keep moving even when you're away from your desk.

This app is for eFixMate staff only. You need an admin account created by your eFixMate administrator to sign in.

QUICK, SECURE SIGN-IN
• Scan the QR code from your profile in the web admin panel, then enter your password
• Or sign in with your personal admin login link
• Two-factor authentication and password re-confirmation for sensitive actions
• You only see the modules your role allows

BOOKINGS IN REAL TIME
• Live booking queue with filters for status, stage, emergency and overdue jobs
• Assign or change the technician for each service, or bulk-assign in one go
• Reschedule visits, resolve missed visits and record cash collected
• Track the technician live on the map, then call or WhatsApp the customer or technician
• Full price, payment, refund and payout breakdown for every booking
• Handle warranty claims and customer complaints from start to finish

TECHNICIANS
• Review new applications section by section: ID documents, selfie and bank details
• Approve, suspend, force offline or assign a manager
• Manage working hours, leave and schedule-change requests
• See every online technician on a live map
• Approve skill-change requests and track referrals

CUSTOMERS
• Customer profiles, addresses, booking history and activity
• Create a booking on a customer's behalf with real available time slots

FINANCE
• Revenue, GST, refunds, settlements and wallet balances at a glance
• Approve, reject or retry refunds
• Run settlement batches and mark technician payouts as paid
• Invoices, failed payments, wallet ledger and financial reports

SETUP & MARKETING
• Service categories, services, skills, charges, taxes, discounts, commissions and coupons
• Countries, states, cities and service areas, including drawing area boundaries on the map
• Area operating hours, time slots, capacity, holidays and price overrides
• Promotions and banners, and push-notification broadcasts to customers or technicians

OPERATIONS & SUPPORT
• Support chat with customers and technicians, including photos
• Website contact inquiries
• SLA monitor for overdue and at-risk work
• Audit logs, report downloads, admin roles and permissions
• Help guides in English and Hindi

Everything you change in the app takes effect immediately, exactly as it would in the web admin panel.
